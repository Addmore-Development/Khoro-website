To self-host an image, download it from unsplash.com and replace the src in the HTML.
Team cards use initials, not stock photos, so real staff portraits can be dropped in later.
Contact form opens the visitor's email app (no server-side script on shared hosting).

Fonts (Google Fonts): Source Serif 4 (headings), Open Sans (body), Montserrat (navigation, buttons, labels).
Colours: black #050505, white, yellow #FFC20E (from the Khoro logo/profile), navy #0C2140 used sparingly.

Sections: full-bleed photo call-to-action bands, an image mosaic for the five focus areas (hover/focus reveals the details), and an editorial "Why clients choose Khoro" layout.
