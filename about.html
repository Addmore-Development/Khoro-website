Founded in 2015, to help Africa’s businesses thrive.

Khoro works to reduce unemployment and poverty in Africa by helping emerging businesses succeed sustainably and act as responsible corporate citizens.

Our Philosophy

A meeting under the trees.

The name Khoro comes from a local African term for a community meeting under trees, where village issues are discussed and plans are made.

  • Lean, Six Sigma, business analysis, operations and creative problem-solving methods combined.
  • Consultants with hands-on entrepreneurial experience and advanced qualifications.
  • Experience with both small businesses and large organizations.
