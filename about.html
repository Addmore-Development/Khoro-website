Helping Africa’s businesses thrive.

Founded in 2015, Khoro works to reduce unemployment and poverty in Africa by helping emerging businesses succeed sustainably and act as responsible corporate citizens.

Our Philosophy

The meaning behind our name.

The name Khoro comes from a local African term for a community meeting under trees, where village issues are discussed and plans are made.
